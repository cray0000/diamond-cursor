Fix a typo without reaching for the arrows.

One wrong letter can send your right hand to the arrow keys and back again. Hold the modifier with your left thumb and you can move the cursor and select the letter with your fingers still on the typing keys.

The same motion works in your browser, editor, and terminal, even when you switch keyboard layouts.
